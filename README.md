# E-Commerce Data Analytics Project 
## Key Business Insights

1. Technology is the highest-performing category in terms of sales and profit.

2. Furniture generates good sales, but its profit performance is comparatively lower than Technology.

3. The West region has the highest sales, while the South region has relatively lower sales.

4. Phones and Chairs are among the major sales-generating sub-categories.

5. Overall sales show an increasing trend over time, with stronger sales in the later years.

## Business Recommendations

1. The business should focus more on high-performing categories and sub-categories such as Technology and Phones by maintaining sufficient inventory and targeted marketing.

2. The business should investigate lower-performing regions and product areas, especially the South region and lower-profit categories, and improve pricing, promotions, and inventory planning.
